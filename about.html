Meet the Founder

The Man behind Kashi Carpets.

Mr. Mahender Kumar Jaiswal

Founder & Master Craftsman · Ramraipur, Bhadohi, Uttar Pradesh, India

Growing up in Bhadohi, Uttar Pradesh—the heart of India's handmade carpet industry—Mr. Mahender Kumar Jaiswal developed a passion for fine craftsmanship and founded Kashi Carpets with a vision to deliver authentic, handcrafted rugs that reflect quality, elegance, and trust.

Surrounded by the rhythmic sound of looms and the artistry of skilled weavers, he developed a deep appreciation for the craftsmanship, dedication, and heritage that define every handmade rug. His commitment to preserving this timeless tradition has shaped Kashi Carpets into a brand that stands for authenticity, excellence, and customer satisfaction.

At Kashi Carpets, every rug is a reflection of uncompromising quality, premium natural materials, and exceptional craftsmanship. Whether creating a bespoke masterpiece, fulfilling large-scale commercial requirements, or helping a family find the perfect statement rug for their home, Mr. Jaiswal personally ensures that every piece meets the highest standards of excellence.

His philosophy is simple—honest craftsmanship, timeless design, transparent service, and lasting customer relationships. Every rug reflects the artistry of Bhadohi's master weavers while embodying the trust, passion, and commitment to quality that define Kashi Carpets, making each creation a timeless investment for generations to come.

Who We Are

Premium rugs, crafted with heart in Bhadohi.

Bhadohi, in Uttar Pradesh, is famously known as India's carpet city. For over a century, artisans of this region have woven some of the finest hand-knotted wool rugs in the world. Kashi Carpets is proud to carry forward this heritage — combining traditional weaving techniques with contemporary designs that suit modern lifestyles.

From the choice of yarn to the final finishing, we oversee every stage in person. This is not mass-manufactured décor — it is art, made to last generations.

💡 Every Kashi Carpet is inspected personally by Mr. Mahender before it leaves our workshop.
