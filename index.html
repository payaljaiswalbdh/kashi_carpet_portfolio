Brand Story

Where every knot tells a story of tradition.

Kashi Carpets is founded on a simple belief — that a rug is more than a floor covering. It is warmth, memory, and craft, woven together thread by thread. Our workshop in Bhadohi carries forward the age-old weaving tradition of Uttar Pradesh's carpet capital.

From earthy browns to deep royal blues, each design is created to complement modern interiors while honouring the timeless character of handloom art.
